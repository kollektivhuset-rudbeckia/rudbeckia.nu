You do not have to live in the house to be part of the collective. As a friend member you may take part in the house's cooking teams and activities, and you collect queue points in the association's internal housing queue.

You get the payment details as soon as you have signed up. If you have any questions, you are welcome to email [ny@rudbeckia.nu](mailto:ny@rudbeckia.nu).

## Levels of membership

Everyone living in the house must be a member of the association and follow its statutes. The association has two levels of membership: resident members and friend members.

### Resident members

Resident members are members of the association who live in the house. Everyone living here who has turned 18 is a resident member, regardless of the type of contract.

### Friend members

Friend members are members of the association who do not live in the house, but who have the right to take part in the house's cooking teams and activities.

## All members

- Apply via the [membership form](https://members.rudbeckia.nu/bli-medlem) or [ny@rudbeckia.nu](mailto:ny@rudbeckia.nu).
- Pay the annual fee between 1 January and the last day of February to keep their membership.
- May take part in the house's dinners.
- Receive **ONE** queue point per year of membership in the association's internal housing queue.
