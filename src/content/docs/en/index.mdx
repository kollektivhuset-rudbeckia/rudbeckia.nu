## Welcome!

The Rudbeckia collective house in the [Rosendal](./rosendal/) district of Uppsala was ready for move-in in November 2021. The house has 42 apartments and more than 300 square metres of shared space. We keep developing the way we live here so that it becomes exactly as wonderful as we want it to be. Every day brings new chances to get to know one another.

:::tip[Curious about living in the collective house?]
- [Contact Rudbeckia's allocation group directly](mailto:ny@rudbeckia.nu) so that we know you are interested!
- [Check available apartments in the Uppsala housing queue](https://www.bostad.uppsala.se/mypages/app/?district=Rosendal&objectType=collective&region=Uppsala)
- [Hemvist Fastigheter's own queue](https://bostad.hemvistforvaltning.se/bostad?cities=Uppsala&page=1)
:::
